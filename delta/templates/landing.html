One change.
Three possible decisions.

Delta compares declared inputs, dependencies, freshness, and implementation identity. Every state includes a reason.

01 / REUSE

Keep completed work

The effective input and implementation still match.

02 / RERUN

Create only the needed job

A relevant input, version, or freshness rule changed.

03 / PENDING

Wait for the real output

A downstream signature cannot be known yet.
